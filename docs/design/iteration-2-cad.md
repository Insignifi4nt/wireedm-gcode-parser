# CAD icon family — iteration 2 review

This is a critical visual and semantic review of the CAD, geometry, and view glyphs in `scripts/icon-source.mjs`. It compares the editable source paths with the generated dark review board and with isolated alternatives exported by `scripts/icon-revisions/cad.mjs`. It does not change the shared icon source or generated assets.

## Verdict

The current set has a useful visual grammar, but it is not one uniform projection system. Solid bodies, imported bodies, body selection, and datum planes use isometric geometry. Sketch primitives and feature commands use orthographic diagrams. That difference is defensible: feature glyphs depict the operation, while body glyphs depict a solid object. Flattening every feature to match the body cube would make the operation vocabulary less direct. Keep that distinction, but make operation diagrams internally consistent and ensure their source geometry is unambiguous.

The biggest semantic weakness is the revolve family member. The current profile is an open bracket and the curved stroke reads like a separate hook; no resulting volume is visible. Loft has a closed outer silhouette but its profile loops are not clearly closed sections. Sweep starts with a closed square, then joins a broad curved strip and a second line, making it unclear which stroke is the profile and which is the trajectory. Those three need another design pass before they become the standard for feature history or toolbar use. Imported-body is also missing the apex and back edges from the solid-body silhouette, so its transfer arrow currently enters what looks like an open container.

At 14–16px, the current pad and pocket remain a strong pair: the outline describes the cut/add shape, and arrow direction differentiates the command. Fillet and chamfer also share a corner and differ by rounded versus beveled treatment. The body/imported-body/select-body isometric family, mirror axis, linear versus circular pattern, and sketch primitive vocabulary remain recognizable. Do not replace them wholesale simply to make the sheet look more uniform.

## Contour and family audit

- The body outline, select-body target, datum plane, pad/pocket profiles, fillet/chamfer corners, shell stock outline, Boolean operands, mirror instances, and pattern instances all contain intended closed silhouettes. Their other open strokes are internal edges, axes, or arrows. Imported-body is the exception: it has a top rim but no closed top-face apex, so it is a misleading open volume.
- Revolve’s current source sketch is open. That is a concrete defect because an operation profile normally needs a closed contour; the proposed profile closes it.
- Loft’s outer path is closed, but its internal profile strokes are open curves rather than visibly closed section loops. The proposed alternatives make the two section loops explicit and connect them.
- Sweep’s square profile is closed. The larger swept boundary is a continuous open path, which is a plausible trajectory/surface edge but is not sufficiently separated visually from the profile. The proposal splits profile, trajectory, and direction into separate paths.
- Polyline and three-point arc endpoints are intentionally open; construction geometry, axes, cursors, dimensions, and camera controls are also line glyphs rather than closed silhouettes. The `circle` helper’s two arc commands end at their starting coordinate without an explicit `Z`; that is visually closed, but writing `Z` into the helper would make closure intent explicit for all circles and dots. These should not be “fixed” by closing every path.
- Fillet/chamfer use the same corner construction and remain distinct at 14, 15, 16, and 24px. Keep that pair. Linear and circular pattern also remain distinct as repeated row versus radial copies. Mirror’s two closed symmetric silhouettes and broken center axis are already clear.
- The isometric cube remains the most coherent depiction of a body. Imported-body adds a transfer arrow to that form; select-body adds a cursor. Preserve those shared geometry primitives. Pad and pocket intentionally use an orthographic section/extrusion diagram, so their flatter look than `body` is not by itself a flaw.

## Proposed path set and review artifacts

`scripts/icon-revisions/cad.mjs` exports 18 isolated `{ id, paths, reason }` alternatives. It includes the highest-ranked operation sketches and exploratory alternatives for imported-body, selection, shell, Boolean, datum, pattern, mirror, and view icons. The alternatives preserve catalog IDs and leave labels, aliases, generated assets, and the shared catalog untouched. Some are explicitly exploratory: reasons identify proposals that need another comparison before integration.

`scripts/icon-revisions/render-cad.mjs` renders the source paths and proposal paths with `@resvg/resvg-js`, the catalog’s 24-unit viewBox, 1.75-unit rounded stroke, and dark review background. It writes exact target-size comparisons for 14, 15, 16, and 24px to `docs/design/icon-review/iteration-2-cad/compare-14.png`, `compare-15.png`, `compare-16.png`, and `compare-24.png`. The full current CAD/geometry/view family appears at 14px and 24px in `family-14.png` and `family-24.png`. The script also checks each proposal’s bounding box and verifies non-empty output at all four sizes.

These are review inputs, not an implementation patch. No changes were made to `scripts/icon-source.mjs`, `src/assets/icons/`, catalog exports, or CAD implementation.
