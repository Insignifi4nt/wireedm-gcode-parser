# General icon design critique, iteration 2

Reviewed 57 icons from actions, navigation, files, storage, status and brand, plus visibility, lock, link, plugin and input-device controls. Rendered their actual paths at 14, 16 and 24 px, with a 48 px construction specimen, in dark and light themes. Selected and disabled specimens use the owning control's color or opacity. This is a visual and semantic review, separate from the earlier runtime review.

The set needs refinement before being called finished. File modifiers do not yet occupy a consistent field; importing and uploading share an upward arrow despite different operations; the busy ring has an unexplained detached stroke; and some names describe concepts the geometry does not depict. Proposed paths are in `scripts/icon-revisions/general.mjs`, with side-by-side renders under `docs/design/icon-review/iteration-2-general/`. Shared production geometry has not been edited.

## Families retained after inspection

Back/forward and up/down are geometric mirrors, with consistent shaft and head dimensions. Undo/redo also mirror correctly. Add/remove, close, confirm and delete retain recognizable simple silhouettes. The save disk is established enough to keep for this technical audience; it remains distinct from copy and revision history at 14 px. History's circular arrow and clock hand stay recognizable in both themes.

Code and JSON pages share the same complete page and fold, with distinct internal marks. JSON braces become small at 14 px but are still different from code chevrons. No extra detail should be added. The browser cache cylinder is recognizable; its compound path intentionally joins side walls to the top and bottom ellipses. Upload and download retain one tray and opposing vertical arrows.

The four panel controls share the exact frame, divider and mirrored chevrons. Visibility remains an eye; fit and zoom controls retain their established metaphors. Status success/error/info share an outer circle, while warning's triangle distinguishes severity before color. Their interior marks are legible at 14 px. Question-mark help remains distinct from information. Lock, link, search and plugin have recognizable contours and do not need speculative redrawing. Settings is a restrained four-lobe cog; additional teeth would make it noisier at these sizes. Adjustment sliders remain a separate, clear family.

Outlined shapes that represent solids, pages, folders, panels, locks and plugin pieces are closed. Curved circular subpaths return to their starting point; they do not leave a visible seam. Deliberately interrupted modified-page outlines, hidden-eye fragments, arrow shafts, slider rails and the busy ring are open. Closing those paths would add unwanted diagonal edges.

## Verification and next decision

`node scripts/icon-revisions/render-general.mjs` renders the 57-icon baseline and 13 proposed refinements, and checks each proposal's stroked bounds against the 24-unit viewBox. The comparison files contain the actual 14/16/24 px specimens, not enlarged stand-ins. Inspected the dark comparison, dark document/status baseline, light control baseline and light revised comparison.

Parent review should adopt the file-family, copy, folder seam and busy-ring corrections first. Evaluate hidden-eye density in the actual control row. Folder persistence, edited-document state and trackpad need contextual decisions. The brand candidate remains an experiment and is the weakest unresolved general-family design problem. These are proposals for another design pass, not approval to publish the whole set.
