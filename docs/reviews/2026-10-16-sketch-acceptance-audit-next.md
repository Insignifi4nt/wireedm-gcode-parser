## Five remaining workflow gaps, in recommended order

1. **Move existing graph edges and sets without reshaping them.** Draw a free rectangle, select its edges or box-select it with a hole, then try moving the set by 10 mm. Edge gestures select only; point movement requires a singleton; **Move profile** requires a legacy profile. Moving corners individually can resize the rectangle instead of translating it. See `CadSketchGraphLayer.tsx:146,173,193`, `CadWorkbench.tsx:741,807` and the singleton guard in `CadSketchGraphLayer.test.tsx:135`. Add one prepared, atomic selected-geometry move with explicit anchor/displacement and retained relations/drivers. Reuse its identity policy for subsequent copy/rotate work. This restores a practical capability that the older contour representation already has. Inventory S03/S07/S08/S19.

2. **Share useful geometry snaps across creation and movement.** With an off-grid endpoint, start a Circle at that point, or move a free point onto a line midpoint. Only Path start/end consult existing graph points; other drawing and graph dragging use grid rounding alone. The snap resolver has no midpoint/on-entity candidates. See `CadSketchCanvas.tsx:320–328`, `CadSketchGraphLayer.tsx:124` and `cadPathPointSnap.ts`. An independent probe at the midpoint of a 20 mm line returns no target. Introduce prepared, screen-distance-ranked endpoint/center/midpoint snaps for these owners, with visible reasons and suppression. Keep coordinate inference distinct from explicitly saved connectivity or constraints. Inventory S09.

3. **Edit existing topology instead of deleting and redrawing it.** A line crossing an arc cannot be split and selectively trimmed; an endpoint cannot extend to a chosen boundary. A typed wall clearance cannot create an exact inset/outset outline. The shared edit union has append/delete/corner operations, but no split/trim/extend/offset contracts (`cadOperations.ts:17–44`). Whole-feature replacement through an agent is not a discoverable topology operation. Start with exact split and explicit surviving-ID/relation rules, then trim/extend; follow with signed line/arc offset and an honest independent/associative policy. Preserve the private-draft and consumer-area repair routes. Inventory S16/S17.

4. **Explain and deliberately consume intersecting regions.** Two radius-5 circles at `(0,0)` and `(6,0)` visibly close, yet a direct production-compiler probe yields zero cycles, both IDs in `arrangementEntityIds`, and `no-material` Pad eligibility. A material branch likewise withholds its component. This is conservative behavior, but the generic message says there are no closed contours. See `cadSketchGraphGeometry.ts:107,140`, `cadSketchSources.ts:12–27` and geometry tests at lines 108/142. Surface the actual unsupported intersection/branch first; then integrate reviewed analytic cells with deliberate region selection and conservative saved-reference invalidation. The isolated arrangement implementation was not audited or enabled here.

5. **Bring supported legacy/reference geometry into deliberate granular workflows.** A newly drawn Slot remains a legacy profile. Points/Edges filters exclude its grips; its center cannot participate in a graph point-pair relation with a newly drawn circle. A reference Curve used by Sweep renders without selectable endpoint grips and edits through segment fields. See `cadFreshSketchGraph.ts:27,53`, `CadSketchCanvas.tsx:629,639–640`, `CadFeatureEditor.tsx:251`, and the explicit legacy-filter test in `CadSketchCanvas.test.tsx:268`. Keep existing sizes/grips and exact imported/plugin sources. Provide revision-owned reference/grip adapters where sufficient; any intentional Slot promotion needs structural tangency and the in-flight radial/equal foundations first. Do not flatten saved groups to obtain apparent parity.
