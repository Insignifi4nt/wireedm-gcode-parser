# Next boundary workflow: middle-arc Trim

2026-10-16, after accepted local `9947e31`. Read-only source/documentation assessment; only this note changed. No tests, native probes, builds or package operations were run.

## Decision

Recommend **middle Trim of an open graph arc**, conditional on a small native preflight before production implementation. Reuse the existing middle-Trim operation and create two ordinary endpoint arcs sharing the original center, with one visible, removable **Equal radius** relation. Defer arc Extend: it needs new full-support angular event ordering and outward-range validation that middle Trim does not need.

Fusion documents middle removal of an open circular arc as producing two curves, and ordinary Extend as retaining the original unless joining/completing a circle changes identity. Onshape also supports arc extension, including a free release location. These are useful interaction references, not requirements to copy automatic merging or constraint transfer. [Fusion Trim API](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_SketchCurve_trim.htm), [Fusion Extend API](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-360-API/files/fusion_SketchCurve_extend.htm), [Onshape Extend](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Sketch/extend.htm)

Neither source asserts our choice to retain a source-side ID or automatically create equality after Trim: those are deliberate app policies.
