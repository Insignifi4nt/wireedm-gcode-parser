# Next user workflows after arc boundary editing

Read-only inventory on 16 October 2026 after accepted arc Extend, against the original [S01–S22 reference](2026-10-16-sketch-workflow-reference.md) and current checklist. Retained radius ownership is the active slice and is not judged complete here. No production changes, tests, probes, browser actions, builds or remote operations were performed.

Selection, Move, snaps, manual Midpoint, dimensions, private consumed-sketch editing, Split/Trim/Extend, detached Offset and intersecting areas are delivered. They are not repeated as missing. The remaining reuse gap is visible in actual code: `cadOperations.ts` has no sketch copy/mirror/rotation or projected-reference edit; the sketch toolbar exposes Move and Offset, while Transform/Mirror/Pattern in `cadDocument.ts` target bodies. `cadSketchProfilePositionSnaps.ts` deliberately returns accepted positions rather than persistent profile references.

## Ordered recommendations

| Priority | Concrete user workflow | Smallest coherent next slice and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw and dimension one bore or mounting detail, then place its counterpart across a construction centerline. | Add **Mirror copy** of selected graph entities about one explicit graph line. Keep it visibly detached: fresh output IDs, preserved shared point identities within the copy, exact line/arc/circle geometry and no source edits. Do not imply a Symmetry relation or editable pattern. Preview both source and result, Cancel without changes, accept once, dimension the new bore independently, build and Undo. This gives a useful S19/precise-copy step before designing persistent instances. |
| 2 | Start from a legacy/DXF/plugin outline, keep the original, and use graph dimensions or local topology tools on an editable copy. | Add an explicit **independent analytic copy** into another sketch with the same resolved frame. Resolve the accepted profile once; allocate owned graph points/entities and preserve exact analytic line/arc/circle geometry. Keep plugin/package and imported source records intact. A rounded slot or plugin outline should copy, accept a local edit, build and save/reopen while the source remains exact. Keep Curve support additive only after its open-chain/frame adapter is covered; no DXF export/reimport workaround. |
| 3 | Reuse a preceding sketch edge as a persistent construction reference, then change or remove its source. | Treat this as a separate S18 ownership slice, beginning with stable graph line/circle IDs and one eligible preceding same-frame sketch. Define recomputation, missing/suppressed-source diagnostics, explicit detach and exact save/reopen before exposing Use/Project. A copied coordinate does not satisfy this workflow. Avoid legacy/plugin boundary ordinals and solid-face references until they have a stable source contract. |

Mirror copy is the recommended immediate slice: a complete everyday result with no new persistent dependency model. Its shared copy primitive can later serve another real consumer; it does not justify a generic transform framework now. Rotate-copy, editable patterns and linked projection remain distinct acceptance work.

## Ownership and data boundaries

- A detached copy must explicitly state what happens to relationships. The smallest policy copies evaluated geometry into new numeric records and leaves every original expression/relation untouched; it must not silently promise copied dimensions, corner ownership or links. Do not partially copy a relation with outside operands. Certify analytic support, signed arc coverage, closure and the exact prepared result; numerical uncertainty rejects rather than distorts the copy.
- Same-position source points are not automatically one identity. Preserve declared source joints/closure and shared centers, but do not proximity-merge independent points. Reflection reverses arc direction. Source/copy/axis IDs and the accepted frame/version must be pinned through preview and Apply; cancellation and private Finish remain under existing shared owners.
- Current graph-cell mode rejects material legacy profiles. Copying a profile beside its still-material original in the same sketch must not silently switch it to construction or change material. Prefer a separate same-frame sketch, or require a deliberate original-construction change. Cross-plane projection can turn circles into unsupported ellipses; reject unsupported frames instead of approximating them.
- New detached geometry fits existing schema-2 graph records; introducing it into schema 1 still follows accepted explicit-edit normalization. A persistent reference or editable pattern adds saved dependency meaning and needs its own compatibility, cache/folder storage and broken-reference policy. Keep source originals and pinned plugin versions exact.

These recommendations use the existing official Use/Mirror/Transform research linked by the reference; no new vendor-parity or universal sketch-completion claim is made.
