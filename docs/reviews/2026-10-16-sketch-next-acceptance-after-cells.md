## Recommended next slice: persistent line midpoint

Add one scalar-free sketch-owned relation, provisionally `{id, kind:'midpoint', pointId, lineId}`, using the existing point/entity selection set, `set-sketch-relation`, preparation, diagnostics and relation form. A mixed selection of exactly one point and one line becomes useful without adding a tool mode. Construction lines are eligible. Start with graph lines only; arc midpoint, legacy/plugin anchors, drawing inference and projection remain outside this contract. Ordinary midpoint snapping must keep its current position-only behavior unless a person explicitly adds the relation.

[Onshape's official Midpoint documentation](https://cad.onshape.com/help/Content/Sketch/midpoint.htm), checked on 16 October 2026, describes selecting a point and a line or arc and constraining the point to the midpoint. Our line-only recommendation is narrower. Installed PlaneGCS declarations expose `p2p_symmetric_ppp`, a candidate for line endpoints symmetric about the selected point. That declaration is not native acceptance evidence. Prove the ordinary mapping through the existing solver before exposing the checkbox; no hidden points, rewritten formulas, JS correction or second solver.

Require independent coordinate residuals against the actual source endpoints under the existing physical-driver policy. Preserve every source scalar and ID, reject endpoint/self/foreign or degenerate operands, and keep incompatible fixed/expression drivers authoritative. Relation removal retains the present coordinates. Split/Trim/deletion must report the actual dependent relation until an explicit transfer policy exists. Never reinterpret a midpoint as a shared endpoint/material joint.

Acceptance should be one coherent part workflow plus a few shared-operation regressions:

1. Place a circle center on a graph construction-line midpoint, add the relation, and change a linked plate-width parameter. The center follows; diameter, exact driver text and unrelated geometry remain unchanged. Include an oblique/reversed line in domain/native checks, not a broad numeric sweep.
2. Cancel and reject an incompatible fixed-center attempt without losing raw input or changing accepted geometry/history. Remove the relation, change the source again and verify deliberate detachment. Undo/Redo restore the exact relation and source records.
3. Complete the centered-bore part through a consumed-sketch Finish, one whole-part Undo, exact human/agent source parity and save/reopen on both adapters. New relation discovery, operands and older-reader rejection must be documented. Existing schemas and records are never silently upgraded.

If the ordinary native mapping cannot satisfy those unchanged checks, stop with that concrete limit. Do not substitute a frozen numeric center or widen precision to make the journey pass. This slice would close one common design-intent gap; it would not complete Tangent, projection, sketch transforms or the full sketch goal.
