Robofil 100 V2 candidate policy record

Recorded candidate programs: prisma-recommended.iso and prisma-exterior-reversed.iso.

The operator-corrected lifecycle emits only G92 at program start. The program selects the
compensation side with G41 or G42, then activates the offset with G38 D0. Linear and
absolute-center circular interpolation use G1, G2, or G3. Program completion emits G40,
G39, and M02 in that order.

When consecutive contours use the same side and the wire remains threaded, G0 moves between
contours without cancelling compensation. The program emits one G41/G42 and G38 D0 pair and
one final G40 and G39 pair. Mixed-side and wire-separation/rethread transitions remain blocked
until an exact Robofil 100 sequence is verified.

The old V2 implementation and the later platform prototype represented manual wire
separation, manual threading, and explicit operator stops with M00. Those three uses
remain unverified on Cristian's Robofil 100 and require graphics simulation, a supervised
dry run, and a new package version if testing changes their command or ordering.

This is an editable test candidate. It is not a claim of machine readiness.
